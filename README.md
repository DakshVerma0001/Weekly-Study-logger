# 📚 Weekly Study Planner, Logger & Habit Tracker

A complete Excel-based productivity system designed to help you plan your week, track your study sessions, build better habits, and monitor your progress through an interactive dashboard.

Everything is connected using formulas, so once the workbook is set up, all you need to do is enter your daily data. The dashboard and analytics update automatically.

---

# Getting Started

Before using the tracker, open the **Settings** sheet. This is where you customize the workbook according to your needs.

You can edit:

- Study Subjects
- Habit Names
- Priority Levels
- Task Status Options
- Weekly Study Goal
- Weekly Task Goal
- Weekly Habit Goal
- Week Start Date

> **Important:** Only edit the highlighted input cells. The remaining cells contain formulas that power the workbook.

Once you've finished setting up the Settings sheet, you're ready to start using the tracker.

---

# How to Use the Workbook

The workbook is designed around a simple weekly workflow.

### Step 1 — Set Up Your Week

Go to the **Settings** sheet and update the **Week Start Date**.

This should be changed once every week, preferably every Monday.

The new week automatically updates the planner, dashboard, and analytics.

---

### Step 2 — Plan Your Week

Open the **Weekly Planner** sheet.

Here you can organize everything you want to accomplish during the week.

For each task, enter:

- Day
- Subject
- Task Name
- Priority
- Estimated Hours
- Deadline
- Status

Use the dropdown menus instead of typing values manually.

Whenever you complete a task, simply change **Completed** to **Yes**.

The row will automatically:

- Turn green
- Apply a strikethrough
- Update the Dashboard
- Update the Progress Analytics

---

### Step 3 — Log Your Study Sessions

After every study session, open the **Daily Study Log**.

Record:

- Date
- Subject
- Topic
- Start Time
- End Time
- Productivity
- Energy Level
- Focus Level
- Notes (Optional)

The workbook automatically calculates your study duration.

As you continue logging sessions, the workbook also calculates:

- Total Daily Study Hours
- Weekly Study Hours
- Average Session Duration
- Longest Study Session
- Average Productivity
- Average Focus Score

No manual calculations are required.

---

### Step 4 — Track Your Habits

Open the **Habit Tracker** at the end of each day.

For every habit, choose one of the available options:

- Yes
- Partial
- No

The tracker automatically applies color coding:

🟢 Yes

🟡 Partial

🔴 No

Based on your entries, the workbook calculates:

- Weekly Completion Percentage
- Success Rate
- Current Streak
- Missed Days

This helps you identify habits that need more consistency.

---

### Step 5 — Monitor Your Progress

Open the **Dashboard** whenever you want to review your performance.

The Dashboard updates automatically using data from every other sheet.

You'll find:

- Weekly KPIs
- Study Progress
- Target vs Actual Study Hours
- Task Completion
- Habit Completion
- Weekly Streak
- Progress Charts
- Visual Performance Summary

There is nothing to enter on this sheet.

Simply use it to understand how your week is going.

---

### Step 6 — Review Your Week

At the end of the week, open the **Progress Analytics** sheet.

This sheet provides a deeper breakdown of your performance.

You'll be able to see:

- Subject-wise Study Hours
- Subject Completion Rate
- Most Studied Subject
- Least Studied Subject
- Best Productivity Day
- Lowest Productivity Day
- Performance Charts
- Weekly Comparisons

Use these insights to improve your planning for the following week.

# Recommended Weekly Workflow

### Every Monday

- Update the Week Start Date
- Plan your weekly tasks

### Every Study Session

- Log your study session immediately after finishing

### Every Evening

- Update your Habit Tracker
- Mark completed tasks

### Throughout the Week

- Check the Dashboard to monitor your progress

### Every Weekend

- Review the Progress Analytics
- Identify weak areas
- Plan improvements for next week

---

# Best Practices

For the best experience:

- Always use the provided dropdown menus.
- Do not rename or delete worksheet headers.
- Avoid editing formula cells.
- If you add more rows, copy the existing formulas and formatting.
- Update your tracker consistently. The dashboard is only as accurate as the data you enter.

---

# Microsoft Excel Compatibility

This workbook is designed for **Microsoft Excel**.

Most features will also work in **Google Sheets**, but some formatting, charts, and formulas may behave differently.

---

# Final Note

This tracker isn't just a planner.

It's a system that helps you understand **how you're actually spending your time**.

Spend a few minutes each day logging your work, and the workbook will handle the rest—giving you clear insights into your consistency, productivity, strengths, and areas that need improvement.
